Work Planner - Your Personal Work Hours Tracker

Developed by @ks-fahad

A simple and efficient application designed to help users track and manage their working hours easily. Track daily working hours, calculate monthly total hours, monitor break time, record overtime hours, and get an approximate salary calculation based on work records. The app provides a clean and user-friendly experience, helping users organize their work details and maintain accurate time records. All data is securely stored in the database, allowing users to access their work history anytime and continue tracking without losing previous information.

A complete solution for professional work tracking, monthly calculations, and better time management.
